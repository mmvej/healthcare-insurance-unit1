Source, limitations and project files

This is a secondary, public teaching dataset: Healthcare Insurance by willian oliveira, version 1, downloaded 27 October 2026. The metadata lists CC0. The original file has seven fields and no missing values. One repeated row is retained because there is no person identifier to prove an error.

The source does not confirm its observation dates, sampling frame, currency or exact geographic boundaries. There are no diagnoses, treatments or enrolment periods. Observed differences are associations. They cannot establish causes, future expenses or appropriate prices or eligibility decisions for individuals.
